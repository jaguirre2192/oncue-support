Support

Getting the most from On Cue

Alarms don’t ring

Open On Cue and check that calendar access and alarms are allowed. If either was turned off, the app shows a button that takes you to Settings. Meetings on calendars you switched off in On Cue’s Settings appear dimmed and won’t ring.

Keeping alarms current

On Cue sets alarms for the coming week and updates them whenever iOS lets it run in the background. For a guaranteed daily update, open Settings in On Cue, tap Set Up a Daily Refresh, choose Shortcuts, tap Add Shortcut, then turn on Automation. You can change its time in the Shortcuts app.

No Join button

On Cue looks for Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Whereby, GoTo, Chime and Slack huddle links in an event’s URL, location, and notes. If a meeting has no Join button, its invite doesn’t include a link On Cue recognizes.
