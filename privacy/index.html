Privacy Policy

On Cue works entirely on your device. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell any personal data.

Calendar

With your permission, On Cue reads the calendars on your device, including accounts such as Google or Outlook that you have added in iOS Settings. It uses event titles, times, and call links found in an event’s URL, location, or notes to schedule alarms and show your upcoming meetings. On Cue never changes or deletes your events, and this information never leaves your device.

Alarms and notifications

On Cue schedules system alarms on your device for upcoming meetings, and may show a local notification when it needs you to open the app so alarms stay current. These are created on your device and are not sent to any server.

Collection and sharing

On Cue has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud service, and makes no network requests of its own. When you tap Join, the meeting link opens in the matching app or in your browser, which is then governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

Your settings, such as alert timing and which calendars to use, are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Shortcuts

If you add the optional “Refresh On Cue Alarms” shortcut, the Shortcuts app runs On Cue on a schedule you control. It refreshes alarms on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Questions

Open a privacy or support request on GitHub . Please don’t include meeting details or links.
